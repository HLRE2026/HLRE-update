# HLRE Homepage Audit & Improvement Recommendations

## Current Homepage Analysis

### Overview
The Hope, Love & Resilience Expedition (HLRE) website tells the compelling story of Mark Ivancic's journey from stroke survivor to expedition leader, documenting a 300km journey through the Canadian Rockies for a documentary project.

## Current Strengths
- **Clear mission statement** in hero section
- **Detailed funding breakdown** showing transparency
- **Compelling personal story** of Mark's recovery
- **Professional design** with good visual hierarchy
- **Contact information** easily accessible

## Critical Issues & Client Concerns

### 1. Hero Section Problems
**Current**: Static group photo with basic text overlay
**Issues**:
- Generic group photo doesn't capture the expedition's essence
- No immediate call-to-action for donations/support
- Missing visual impact that shows the journey's scale
- Text could be more compelling and action-oriented

**Recommended Changes**:
- Replace with dynamic hero image showing the actual Rocky Mountain terrain
- Add prominent "Support the Expedition" CTA button
- Include a brief video preview or motion graphics
- Stronger headline emphasizing transformation and impact

### 2. Missing Prominent Video Content
**Current**: Main research video buried in Resources section
**Client Need**: Feature the main video immediately on homepage

**Recommended Changes**:
- Embed primary expedition video directly below hero section
- Create video thumbnail with clear play button
- Add video description explaining expedition purpose
- Consider auto-playing preview (muted) to grab attention

### 3. Unclear Value Proposition for Sponsors
**Current**: Funding allocation shown but lacks context
**Client Concern**: Sponsors need clearer understanding of impact beyond documentary

**Recommended Changes**:
- Add "Why This Matters" section prominently below hero
- Create impact metrics section showing:
  - Community members reached through Mark's speaking
  - Educational institutions involved
  - Long-term documentary distribution plans
  - Community outreach programs funded
- Visual infographics showing fund allocation with explanations

### 4. Weak Messaging Hierarchy
**Current**: Story flows well but lacks urgency
**Issues**:
- No clear next steps for visitors
- Missing emotional hooks early in the experience
- Funding goal feels buried in statistics

**Recommended Changes**:
- Add progress bar showing current funding status
- Include testimonials from community members
- Create urgency with countdown to expedition date
- Add "How You Can Help" section with multiple support options

## Detailed Recommendations by Section

### Hero Section Redesign
```
CURRENT:
- Group photo background
- Basic headline and subtitle
- No CTA buttons

PROPOSED:
- Panoramic Rocky Mountain landscape image
- Motion graphics or subtle video background
- Stronger headline: "Transform Lives Through the Power of Resilience"
- Subtitle: "Join Mark's 300km journey documenting hope and recovery"
- Dual CTAs: "Support the Expedition" and "Watch Our Story"
```

### New Sections to Add

#### 1. "Why This Expedition Matters" (immediately after hero)
- Brief explanation of mission beyond documentary
- Impact on communities
- Educational value
- Support for stroke survivors and recovery communities

#### 2. "Featured Video" (primary position)
- Embed main research video with professional thumbnail
- Clear description of video content
- Related video links

#### 3. "Impact & Transparency" (enhanced funding section)
- Visual breakdown of fund usage
- Timeline of project milestones
- Community partnerships
- Educational outreach plans

#### 4. "How You Can Help"
- Multiple support options beyond financial
- Social media sharing
- Community partnerships
- Volunteer opportunities

### Visual Improvements Needed

#### Hero Image Options:
1. **Panoramic Rocky Mountain vista** - Shows scale and beauty of journey
2. **Mark in action on previous expeditions** - Personal connection
3. **Split image** - Before/after or preparation/journey
4. **Video background** - Subtle motion of mountain landscapes

#### Color Scheme Enhancement:
- Current earth tones work well
- Add more vibrant accent colors for CTAs
- Ensure high contrast for accessibility

#### Typography Improvements:
- Larger, bolder headlines
- Better spacing between sections
- Consistent font weights

## Technical Recommendations

### Performance Optimizations:
- Optimize images for web delivery
- Implement lazy loading for below-fold content
- Compress video content for faster loading

### Mobile Responsiveness:
- Ensure hero video works on mobile devices
- Test CTA button placement on smaller screens
- Optimize image sizes for different devices

### SEO Improvements:
- Add meta descriptions focusing on expedition goals
- Include alt text for all images
- Optimize page titles for search visibility
